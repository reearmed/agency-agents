# Revenue workflows

## Portfolio focus

Keep NOREVYO as the compounding product asset. Use automation services as a controlled cash-flow experiment. Build digital products only from repeated, evidenced customer pain. Do not launch all three simultaneously.

## Workflow A — NOREVYO growth loop

1. Analytics Reporter reads approved product and funnel metrics.
2. Opportunity Researcher reviews customer questions, public competitor positioning and approved feedback.
3. Growth Hacker identifies one bottleneck and proposes a measurable experiment.
4. Product Sprint Prioritizer ranks impact, confidence, effort, risk and strategic fit.
5. Content Creator drafts supporting copy and educational content.
6. Backend Architect/DevOps prepare a development-only change if needed.
7. Reality Reviewer checks metric definitions, evidence, test coverage and rollback.
8. Operator approves any customer-facing release or publication.

North-star candidates: qualified opportunities captured per active site, visitor-to-qualified-lead conversion, time-to-first-response, activation-to-value and paid retention. Define exact formulas and source tables before reporting; never invent a baseline.

## Workflow B — productized AI automation services

1. Researcher selects one narrow vertical and problem using public evidence.
2. Growth Hacker defines an outcome-based offer and disqualifiers.
3. Finance Tracker models setup effort, support burden, gross margin and delivery capacity.
4. Legal Compliance Checker reviews claims, data handling, contracts and outreach requirements.
5. Outbound Strategist prepares a small, manually reviewable prospect list with source and relevance rationale.
6. Content Creator prepares a personalized, truthful message draft.
7. Operator reviews every recipient and message before any send.
8. Delivery team uses a written scope, acceptance criteria, data-processing terms and change control.

Do not mass-email. For Canadian commercial electronic messages, assess CASL applicability, consent basis, sender identification and unsubscribe requirements before any outreach. Maintain suppression lists and honor opt-outs.

## Workflow C — digital product validation

1. Collect repeated problems from lawful, public sources and approved customer feedback.
2. Score urgency, willingness to pay, reachable audience, competition and build effort.
3. Interview or test demand manually with operator-approved outreach.
4. Create a no-code or low-cost prototype and a clear landing-page draft.
5. Seek explicit operator approval before publication, payment collection or paid promotion.
6. Ship a small version only after demand evidence; measure conversion, refunds, support load and margin.
7. Stop if pre-defined evidence thresholds are not met.

## Daily operating cycle

- **Morning:** queue health, spend, urgent issues, funnel movement, top three opportunities.
- **Throughout day:** bounded research and drafting jobs; engineering tasks only in isolated development branches.
- **Evening:** reconcile completed tasks, verify evidence, summarize pending approvals, archive artifacts.
- **Weekly:** portfolio review; decide continue, change or stop for each experiment.

## Executive report format

1. Revenue and pipeline by business unit (actuals only, with source and period).
2. Product/funnel metrics and trend.
3. Experiments: hypothesis, result, confidence and guardrail.
4. Top opportunities with evidence and expected effort.
5. Risks, incidents, compliance and spend.
6. Decisions required from operator.
7. Next week's one primary objective and one optional experiment.

## KPI definitions to configure

| Area | Metric | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS | MRR / ARR | Reconcile against billing source; distinguish booked, collected and recurring |
| SaaS | Activation | Explicit event and cohort window; no vanity proxy |
| SaaS | Qualified opportunity rate | Qualified leads / eligible visitors, with qualification rubric |
| Services | Qualified pipeline | Prospect value × stage probability; disclose assumptions |
| Services | Delivery gross margin | Collected revenue less direct delivery cost |
| Products | Paid conversion | Paid customers / eligible unique visitors |
| Portfolio | Cash burn | Actual outflows by category and period |
| Automation | Cost per successful job | Provider and infrastructure cost / verified successful jobs |

No metric should be populated until its source, grain, timezone, freshness and formula are documented.
