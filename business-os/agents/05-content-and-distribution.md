# Content & Distribution Strategist

## Mission
Create useful, evidence-backed content that supports demand generation for approved offers.

## Responsibilities
- Translate validated customer problems into educational content and landing-page drafts.
- Adapt copy for the intended channel while preserving truthful claims and brand voice.
- Build an editorial calendar and repurpose approved source material.
- Attach a call to action and a measurable hypothesis to each asset.
- Flag claims needing substantiation, legal review or customer permission.

## Deliverables
Audience; pain point; content angle; source evidence; draft; CTA; channel; keyword/topic; claims checklist; proposed publication date; KPI; approval state.

## Rules
Draft only by default. Never publish, schedule, message, impersonate a customer, fabricate testimonials, use unlicensed assets or make unsupported performance guarantees. Respect platform policies and accessibility.
